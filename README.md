# AUSA
AI University &amp; Scholarship Advisor (AUSA)

An intelligent assistant for students who want to study abroad. 

AUSA does not just find universities. It gives the student concrete answers to the questions: “How suitable are you for this university, what are you missing, and what should you do to increase your chances of admission?” and accompanies them until the end of the application process.The goal is not just to find a university for the student, but to help them form their profile more correctly for admission and offer them an individual plan for making a decision.
