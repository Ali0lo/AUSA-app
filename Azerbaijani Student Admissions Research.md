# **International Admissions Infrastructure and Azerbaijan State Grant Governance**

## **System Alignment and Portal Verification for International Admissions**

Evaluating foreign undergraduate admission pathways for Azerbaijani applicants holding the 11-year *Orta təhsil haqqında attestat* (Certificate of Complete Secondary Education) requires a strict distinction between direct admission pathways, foundation requirements, and national institutional frameworks. Higher education systems across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East vary significantly in how secondary qualifications are evaluated, language requirements are verified, and visa regulations are enforced.

### **Hong Kong SAR Higher Education Governance vs. Mainland China**

A common structural misconception in study-abroad modeling is treating Hong Kong higher education institutions as functionally identical to mainland Chinese universities. Admissions, language policy, immigration governance, and fee structures in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR) operate under a distinct framework from mainland China. Under the "One Country, Two Systems" policy, the University of Hong Kong, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and The Hong Kong Polytechnic University maintain complete institutional autonomy. International applicants apply through dedicated Non-JUPAS (Joint University Programmes Admissions System) international channels rather than the mainland Chinese GaoKao or mainland international applicant portals. Medium of instruction across these Hong Kong institutions is English, making Chinese language proficiency scores (HSK) irrelevant for general undergraduate entry; applicants must instead present English language test scores (IELTS or TOEFL). Immigration is administered independently by the HKSAR Immigration Department via a Hong Kong Student Visa, rather than the mainland Chinese X-visa. Tuition fees are set independently on a Hong Kong Dollar (HKD) scale, reflecting a higher cost structure than mainland Renminbi (RMB) tuition rates.

## **Azerbaijan State Grant Governance: The "Dövlət Proqramı" Framework**

### **Operational Status and Executive Enabling Framework**

The Azerbaijani state-funded study-abroad program is **active and accepting applications for study commencing in the 2026 academic cycle**8. The original 2007–2015 scholarship scheme has officially concluded12. The active successor scheme in force is designated as:  
> **“Gənclərin xarici ölkələrin nüfuzlu ali təhsil müəssisələrində təhsil almalarına dair 2022–2028-ci illər üçün Dövlət Proqramı”**  
> \[cite: 10, 11, 13, 14\]  
> *(State Program for the Education of Youth in Prestigious Foreign Higher Education Institutions for 2022–2028)*10.  
This scheme was established pursuant to Decree No. 3163 issued by the President of the Republic of Azerbaijan on February 28, 202214. Administrative execution is overseen by the Ministry of Science and Education of the Republic of Azerbaijan through its dedicated program management authority (dp.edu.az)16. Official announcements confirm that application selection procedures for the 2026/2027 academic year are operating under this decree8.

### **Bachelor Level Academic Thresholds**

For candidates applying for state funding at the undergraduate (Bachelor) level, academic eligibility requires participation in the centralized entrance examinations administered by the State Examination Centre of the Republic of Azerbaijan (DİM \- *Dövlət İmtahan Mərkəzi*)19. The mandatory academic score threshold varies depending on the target field of study19.  
For applicants in Group 1 (Engineering and Technology specializations), candidates must achieve a minimum entrance score of 400 points20. Specifically, for specializations including Civil Engineering (*İnşaat mühəndisliyi*), Electronics Engineering (*Elektronika mühəndisliyi*), and Electrical Engineering (*Elektrik mühəndisliyi*), the required minimum score is 400 points21. For all other eligible priority fields, the academic score threshold is set at a minimum of 550 points19.  
The official regulation establishes this threshold:  
> "yekun olaraq ən azı 400, digər sahələr üzrə ən azı 550 bal toplaması."  
> \[cite: 19\]  
> *(...achieving a final score of at least 400, and at least 550 points for other fields.)*  
> \[cite: 19\]  
This requirement is further detailed in published candidate guidelines:  
> "DİM imtahanlarında iştirak etmiş 1-ci ixtisas qrupunun abituriyentləri və 400 və ya daha yüksək bal toplamış namizədlər iştirak edə bilər."  
> \[cite: 20\]  
> *(...applicants from the 1st specialty group who participated in DİM examinations and obtained 400 or higher points may participate.)*  
> \[cite: 20\]  
Direct exemption from the DİM examination requirement applies solely to medalists and winners of recognized international subject olympiads19.

### **Eligibility Framework for Postgraduate Levels (Master's and PhD)**

The secondary school DİM entrance examination threshold of 400–550 points **does not apply** to Master's or PhD applicants22. Eligibility for postgraduate levels is governed by separate academic standards:

* **Master's Level (*Magistratura*)**: Applicants are evaluated based on their undergraduate cumulative grade point average (GPA or equivalent grade percentage), academic performance records, and securing an unconditional offer of admission from an approved host institution within an officially designated priority field23.  
* **PhD Level (*Doktorantura*)**: Candidates must hold a recognized Master's degree (or equivalent qualification) and obtain an unconditional offer of admission to a doctoral program at an approved university within a designated priority research domain22. High school DİM scores are excluded from postgraduate evaluations22.

### **Language Proficiency Requirements**

Applicants must satisfy the language proficiency requirements stipulated by the medium of instruction at their target foreign university25. Candidates must either meet the host institution's language requirements directly through unconditional admission or present certified international test results meeting state program criteria25. Where English is the primary language of instruction, recognized minimum scores include a TOEFL iBT total score of at least 80 (with subscores of at least 20 in each component), a Duolingo English Test score of 110 or higher, or equivalent IELTS Academic bands required by the host university's offer letter25.

### **Age Limits**

Official regulations under Presidential Decree No. 3163 do not publish an explicit upper numerical age limit for program applicants14. Eligibility requires full legal capacity and applying to undertake a degree level higher than any degree currently held.

### **Approved Country and University Lists**

The Ministry of Science and Education issues an updated list of target universities and programs (*xaricdə təhsil üzrə proqramların və ali təhsil müəssisələrinin siyahısı*) annually17. Across active implementation cycles, the approved listing includes up to 151–179 higher education institutions across 21–28 countries, covering 15 strategic priority sector fields (such as Information Technology, Engineering, Agriculture, Public Health, and Basic Sciences)16.  
The target catalogue varies by degree level17:

* **Bachelor Level**: Restricted to specific technical, engineering, and dual-degree tracks (such as joint international university programs) at designated institutions17.  
* **Master's and PhD Levels**: Encompasses a broader list of global institutions (typically those ranked within top global tiers in QS or THE subject rankings) across all 15 official priority fields17.

### **Annual Funded Quotas and Allocations**

The program maintains an official annual funding capacity of up to **500 scholars per year**8. For the 2026/2027 academic intake, the target distribution of funded places across degree levels is allocated as follows8:

| PhD / Doctoral | Doktorantura | 228 |

### **Financial Coverage Structure**

Funding for the State Program is financed through the State Oil Fund of the Republic of Azerbaijan (SOFAZ) under the administrative direction of the Ministry of Science and Education18. The scholarship grant covers costs associated with overseas study18:

* **Tuition Fees**: Direct payment of host university tuition costs disbursed directly to the educational institution18.  
* **Living Allowance**: Monthly stipends calculated based on local cost-of-living standards in the host country to cover accommodation, food, and living expenses18.  
* **Health Insurance**: Full coverage of mandatory foreign health and medical insurance costs18.  
* **International Travel**: Annual allowance covering one round-trip international transit ticket between Azerbaijan and the host study destination per academic year18.  
* **Visa and Legal Stay Fees**: Direct reimbursement of consular visa fees and local residence permit registration expenses18.

### **Obligations and Post-Graduation Work Requirements**

Recipients of the State Program enter into a binding tripartite agreement with the Ministry of Science and Education12. A key contractual condition dictates a mandatory post-graduation employment period in Azerbaijan12. Graduates are legally required to return to the Republic of Azerbaijan and complete **5 years of work activity** (*5 il əmək fəaliyyəti*)12.  
The official text governing graduate service mandates this condition:  
> "Proqramın şərtlərinə görə, təhsilini bitirdikdən sonra məzunlar ölkəyə qayıdıb 5 il əmək fəaliyyəti ilə məşğul olmalıdır."  
> \[cite: 12\]  
> *(According to the conditions of the Program, after completing their education, graduates must return to the country and engage in labor activity for 5 years.)*  
> \[cite: 12\]  
Non-compliance with contractual provisions—such as failure to return to Azerbaijan, failure to complete the academic program, or breach of the 5-year work requirement—requires full reimbursement to the State of all expended funds, alongside administrative interest and legal penalties specified in the contract.

### **Application Window and Submission System**

Applications for the State Program are processed online through the official state portal (portal.edu.az), with program guidelines published on the official site (dp.edu.az)17. The electronic application window opens annually during the first quarter of the calendar year (typically January or February) and closes between April and June preceding the start of the target academic year27. Applicants submit verified academic transcripts, unconditional admission offers from approved foreign institutions, language proficiency documentation, and DİM examination results through the digital portal prior to proceeding to panel interviews23.
